Place the model checkpoints here (`bash scripts/download_weights.sh`):

- `svd.safetensors` (Stable Video Diffusion, 14 frames)
- `sd-v1-5-inpainting.ckpt` or `sd-v1-5-inpainting.safetensors`

ZoeDepth, its MiDaS backbone and the OpenCLIP image encoder of SVD are
downloaded automatically on the first run.
